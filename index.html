inbox-zero

inbox-zero is a private, single-user tool that keeps the owner's own email filters tidy and in sync across their Gmail and Outlook mailboxes.

It signs in to the owner's own Google account, with the owner's explicit consent, and uses the Gmail API only to create, list and delete that account's email filters and labels. It cannot read, send or delete email.

It is not offered to anyone else, has no other users, and collects no data from visitors to this site.
